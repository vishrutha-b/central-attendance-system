Central Attendance Management System (CAMS)

Complete Operational Specification, Architectural Guide & User Manual

1. Executive Summary & Objective

The Central Attendance Management System (CAMS) is an automated backend service designed to record employee working hours, enforce corporate presence policies, manage leaves, and provide instant alert dispatching.

Core Capabilities:
  • Deterministic Policy Rules: Clear math-based thresholds without ambiguity.
  • Instant Daily Alert (< 5 hrs): Employees who clock out before completing 5 hours receive an immediate automated email alert upon exit.
  • Weekly WFH & Office Enforcement: Minimum 3 days/week, minimum 15 hours/week, and 5 hours/day average.
  • Self-Service Leave Protection: Approved leaves excuse employees from attendance requirements, preventing violation notices.

2. Attendance Policy Specification

Policy Requirement Standard Threshold Evaluation Frequency Non-Compliance Action
Mandatory Daily Hours ≥ 5.0 Hours/Day Immediate (On Exit) Instant Email Alert to Employee
Mandatory Weekly Days ≥ 3 Days/Week Weekly Audit Close Flagged in Weekly Notice
Mandatory Weekly Hours ≥ 15.0 Hours/Week Weekly Audit Close Flagged in Weekly Notice
Daily Average Hours ≥ 5.0 Hours/Day Weekly Audit Close Flagged in Weekly Notice
Approved Leave Days Excused Proportional Deduction No Penalty (Thresholds Reduced)

5. Key Workflows & Swagger Testing Guide

A. Daily Check-in & Exit Before 5 Hours (< 5.0 hrs)

  1. Employee Check-In at 09:00:
    Call POST /api/v1/attendance/entry with: 
    { "employee_id": "EMP-007", "entry_time": "09:00" }
  2. Employee Check-Out at 12:00 (3 hours completed):
    Call POST /api/v1/attendance/exit with: 
    { "employee_id": "EMP-007", "exit_time": "12:00" }
  3. Result:
    • Session saved successfully with total_hours: 3.0 and status complete.
    • Response returns: "policy_alert": "Policy non-compliance: 3.00 hrs is less than mandatory 5.0 hrs/day. Automated alert email dispatched..."
    • The employee receives an official email alert immediately.

B. Single-Click Quick Record

Call POST /api/v1/attendance/quick-record:

{
  "employee_id": "EMP-007",
  "date": "2026-10-09",
  "entry_time": "09:00",
  "exit_time": "12:00"
}

C. Employee Self-Service Leave Request

  1. Employee Submits Leave (No Admin token required):
    Call POST /api/v1/leave with: 
    {
  "employee_id": "EMP-007",
  "leave_type": "sick",
  "start_date": "2026-10-12",
  "end_date": "2026-10-13",
  "reason": "Doctor appointment"
}
  2. Manager Approves:
    Call PATCH /api/v1/leave/{leave_id}/approve with note: {"note": "Approved"}.
  3. Protection: Approved leave days are automatically excused by the policy engine, reducing required weekly days and hours proportionally so no violation email is triggered.
