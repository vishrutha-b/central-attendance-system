Phase 1: Introduction & The Problem Statement (1 - 2 mins)

🗣️ What to Say (Word-for-Word):
"Good morning / afternoon everyone.

Today, I am presenting the Central Attendance Management System (CAMS) — an enterprise-grade backend service built with Python 3.12, FastAPI, and asynchronous MongoDB.

In modern hybrid work models, companies mandate that employees attend the office at least 3 days a week, complete a minimum of 15 hours per week, and maintain at least a 5-hour daily presence.

Traditional HR systems only detect attendance shortfalls at the end of the month during payroll processing. By then, it is too late to correct.

Our system solves this with instant, real-time enforcement:
1. If an employee logs in and exits before completing the mandatory 5.0 hours, our backend detects the deficit the exact minute they clock out and dispatches an executive alert email to that person immediately.
2. At the end of the week, an automated auditor evaluates the 3 days / 15 hours requirement.
3. And importantly, our system includes a smart leave adjustment engine: approved leaves and company holidays automatically lower weekly targets so excused employees never receive wrongful violation notices.

Let's take a quick look at the codebase architecture, and then I will demonstrate the live email delivery."

Phase 2: Codebase Architecture & File Walkthrough (2 mins)

🖥️ Action: Switch to VS Code and expand the project tree.
🗣️ What to Say:
"The project follows a clean 3-tier enterprise architecture:

• app/main.py: The FastAPI application entrypoint using the modern lifespan context manager to manage database connection pools gracefully and ensure indexes are automatically built on startup.
• app/api/v1/: Contains our RESTful routers:
  - attendance.py: Endpoints for clock-in (/entry), clock-out (/exit), and single-click combined sessions (/quick-record).
  - leave.py: Allows employees to submit leave requests without needing admin credentials, plus admin approvals.
  - employees.py: Full employee lifecycle CRUD operations.
  - reports.py: Unified attendance compliance reports and downloadable CSV spreadsheets.
• app/services/: Houses our core business rules:
  - attendance_service.py: Computes session durations and immediately evaluates the 5-hour threshold on exit.
  - policy_service.py: Deterministic rules engine auditing the 3-day / 15-hour policy with leave scaling.
  - notification_service.py: Dispatches multi-part MIME emails with executive HTML templates and plain text fallbacks.
• app/templates/: Houses daily_violation.html and weekly_violation.html for corporate email branding.
• app/database/: Built strictly with PyMongo Async (AsyncMongoClient) for non-blocking I/O without heavy ORMs.
• tests/: Validated with 39 automated tests covering security, token authentication, and email triggers with 100% pass rate."

"To ensure notifications reach real corporate inboxes reliably, we configured SMTP using Gmail's secure relay:

1. In .env, we set EMAIL_DELIVERY_MODE=smtp, pointing to smtp.gmail.com on port 587 with TLS enabled.
2. For authentication, Google blocks standard account passwords. Instead, we generate a dedicated 16-digit Google App Password:
  • We enable 2-Step Verification on the Google Account.
  • Under App Passwords, we create a dedicated 16-character token for 'CAMS Attendance' and set it in .env.
  • This allows the backend to safely dispatch TLS-encrypted emails without exposing personal credentials.

Q&A Cheatsheet (Common Questions & Winning Answers)

QuestionWinning Technical Answer
Why PyMongo Async instead of Motor? Motor has deprecation warnings on newer Python versions. PyMongo Async (AsyncMongoClient) is the official native asynchronous driver from MongoDB without heavy ORM overhead.
What if an employee clocks in and out multiple times a day? CAMS aggregates all completed sessions for that date. If cumulative active hours reach 5.0 hours, they are fully compliant.
How are duplicate emails prevented during audits? We use an idempotent deduplication key in notification_logs formatted as {employee_id}::{week_start}::violation. If already sent, it skips duplicate emails.
Can this connect to biometric turnstiles? Yes. Turnstiles can hit POST /api/v1/attendance/entry and /exit directly on badge swipe, or batch upload historical logs via /quick-record.
